Note on Intentional Binding

Unlike this task, Intentional Binding specifically compares the perceived time of action to the perceived time of an external effect (like a beep).

What are we measuring?

In the 1980s, Benjamin Libet used this clock to measure the timing of the conscious "urge" to move. He was looking for the W-judgment — the time subjects reported becoming aware of their intention.

The Conclusion

"The brain 'decides' to initiate or, at the least, prepare to initiate the act at a time before there is any reportable conscious awareness that such a decision has taken place."

Terminology Guide

RP

Readiness Potential

Neural activity in the SMA starting ~550ms before movement.

W

Awareness of Urge

Conscious intention, reported ~200ms before movement.

M

Awareness of Movement

The feeling of the physical body actually starting to move.
